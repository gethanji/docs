# Workspace insights

Open **Activity** from the workspace title bar. It belongs to the workspace,
so opening it does not switch you through Document or require Document to be on.
Return to the product you were using when you are ready.

The page opens a usable shell first, then loads the scoped history. A first visit
can take longer while repositories are read. Loading has a visible state, a
bounded wait and a retry action; later visits generally reuse warm data. Refresh
requests current history without replacing the document or losing your filters.

![Workspace insights with fictional contributions](assets/activity-insights.png)

## Read the overview

The overview combines recorded contributions, a seven-day comparison, active
days and the busiest day. Product bars show where activity happened, and the
human/agent split shows the recorded attribution. A chart describes contributions
in the journal, **not hours worked, time saved or a productivity score**. A quiet
chart can mean unrecorded work, not inactivity.

Click a product to narrow the journal. Product, person and human/agent filters
are bookmarkable. Filtering and browser Back update the same page. When a history
read is capped, the interface says so; a partial count is not presented as a
complete history.

## Follow the journal

Entries group by day and describe changes in documents, issues and canvases,
alongside recorded agent work. Runs and sessions can fold related actions into
one readable entry. Follow an entry to the work it describes; choose Older to
continue through history. The overview and the journal serve different scales:
a recent pattern above, the events behind it below.

Each row is scoped to what you can read. A workspace-wide view does not grant
access to another person’s private pages, trackers or canvases. Journal responses
are private and are not a public feed or part of a static documentation export.
