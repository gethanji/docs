# Using Hanji

This section is the how-to. It is written to be read in order the first time, and dipped into after.

1. [[Install Hanji]] gets it running on your machine in a handful of commands.
2. [[Mounts]] points it at the repositories you want to read and write.
3. [[Reading and writing]] covers the human front: reading, editing, history, and making new pages.
4. [[Agents]] connects a coding agent through the MCP server, with scoped tokens and propose-as-PR.
5. [[Proposals]] is where those agent suggestions land: review the diff, merge or reject.
6. [[On your tailnet]] moves the instance onto your tailnet, where the network signs people in and nobody types a password.
7. [[Command reference]] is the full list of CLI commands, for when you already know what you want.

A note before you start. Hanji is early. It runs today on your machine: one owner, the people you add, and the agents you scope - and the recommended way to share it with a team is [[On your tailnet]]. Hosted sync sits in the later column of [[Where we are]], not in your hands yet. What is here works, and this section documents only what works.
