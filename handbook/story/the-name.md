# The name

한지, hanji, is traditional Korean handmade paper. The name gives the research lab a material to think with: a thin surface that takes different forms according to what people need to do.

A document, a conversation, a project canvas. Different surfaces for the same question: how can people and agents work well together?

## A surface for the interaction

Hanji's role is the layer between a person and an agent. The interface helps make context, contributions and choices understandable, leaving room for a person to question the work and decide what happens next.

The paper metaphor carries that idea into the product. The surface should be calm enough to read and think on, flexible enough to suit the interaction, and quiet enough to keep attention on the work.

## Many forms, one research direction

Think, Plan and Document are current expressions of that approach. They can change as we learn. The name belongs to the wider research into human–agent interaction, beyond any single app or arrangement of features.

Care, craft and portable content matter to the products too. [[The visual language]] describes the material choices; [[Mission and vision]] explains the research behind them.
