The brand

Hanji is a research lab for human–agent interaction. Its central idea is a thin surface between people and agents, bringing enough clarity for people to understand, question and direct the work.

The lab grew from an individual need, through countless iterations. It now shares results publicly and as open source while continuing to explore. The brand should carry that curiosity and care, alongside the practical quality of working tools.

  • The name gives us the paper metaphor: one material, many forms of interaction.
  • The visual language gives the work a calm, readable surface, with editorial typography, restrained color and the ㅎ mark.
  • Voice explains how to write about the research plainly and distinguish working capabilities from ambitions.

The lab and its products

Lead with the research question and its purpose: clearer interaction and sound human judgment. Then show the workspace as a current expression of that research. Think, Plan and Document deserve clear product explanations, without making their present arrangement the definition of Hanji.

Open formats and ownership support the work. They should be explained as product principles and capabilities. The mission is better interaction with our virtual companions and colleagues.

The posture

Thoughtful, practical and open to change. Show the actual work and explain what it helps someone understand. Describe where an idea came from, what the current tool does and what remains uncertain. Sharing a result is an invitation to use it and learn from it together.

The visual craft should support clarity. A logo treatment or a setting can be considered carefully without becoming the reason the product exists.
